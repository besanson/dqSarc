## A. Retroactive disclosure — prompt variant (h1-full)

The original prereg did not pin `prompt_variant`. The runner defaulted to `naive`
(no ordering formula), under which the agent is price-inelastic (Phase 0a elasticity
0.000) and the null ADR is a decision-competence artifact, not a data-quality result.
After observing that null, the experiments were switched to `policy_instructed` — the
variant Phase 0b/0c created to remove the competence confound (elasticity 0.976 /
0.992; supplies the newsvendor policy, **no** data-quality language, decider stays
metadata-blind). Switching after seeing the null is a **deviation**; it is justified
by the pre-existing Phase 0b/0c lineage and disclosed here and in FINDINGS §2a. Both
readings are reported: the `naive` uniform-null (competence shield) and the
`policy_instructed` result.

## C. Rate-axis redesign

The corruption draw is now rate-dependent (`sarc_dq.substrate.corruption_decision`),
fixing the nested/duplicate cells of the pre-fix sampler (FINDINGS §6). On top of that:

- **H1 (`h1-full`, `h1-ladder`) and H2 (`h2-detection`)**: each cell corrupts a
  **fixed `n_corrupted = 25`** episodes; `rate` becomes a **stratification label**,
  not a prevalence. Rationale: these read per-corrupted metrics (ADR, detection);
  an equal, adequate n per cell gives stable estimates and removes the low-rate
  degeneracy (n=2) that a true-rate axis produces at 0.02.
- **H3 (`h3-frontier`)**: keeps **true rates**. Rationale: H3's headline is the
  loss-avoided vs. false-block frontier, and false-block economics are driven by
  corruption *prevalence* — fixing n would erase the axis the hypothesis needs.
- **H4 (`h4-recovery`), ablations**: keep **true rates** (they also involve
  blocking/false-block economics on the clean cells; not named for fixed-n in the
  governing instruction). Revisit only by a further dated addendum.

Config stamps `sampling` (`fixed_n` | `rate`) and `fixed_n`. A summary's sampling
design must match this table or it is INVALID.

## D. No new predicates before H4 (W5)

The DQ gate is **frozen as-is** for the H4/H3 re-runs. No predicate may be added,
removed, or re-parameterised before those runs. The `silent_unit_change` and
`plausible_outlier` coverage gaps (H2, FINDINGS §5/W4) are **reported as results**,
not patched. A unit-consistency predicate is documented as **v1.1 future work only**.

The sampler fix changes the deterministic mock matrix, so

no timestamp). This re-freeze is a correctness consequence of C, not a result change;
`make gigo-verify` reproduces it exactly. The original freeze encoded the sampler bug.

## F. Elevated result — the naive-null h1 run

The `$13.12` `naive`-prompt `h1-full` run is elevated to a **reported result**: the
incompetence shield replicated at matrix scale — a price-inelastic frontier decider
converts injected corruption to a material order defect in **0%** of cells across all
eight classes and four rates. Being a uniform zero, it is insensitive to the sampler
nesting (FINDINGS §6) and is retained; it will still be re-run under the fixed sampler
for uniformity before final sign-off.
